01 · Overview

Managing a business card account involves more than making a payment. Customers need to understand what they owe, review the transactions behind their balance, identify fees and rebates, and know when action is required.

I worked on an experience that connects these financial activities around a clearer statement journey. The goal was to help customers understand their account, find the details behind a balance, and move confidently from review to payment.

02 · The challenge

Billing information can be difficult to understand when statements, transactions, payments and account details feel like separate pieces. The opportunity was to create a simple, connected experience without losing the financial detail customers need to manage their business.

03 · Key decisions

Where the work focused

01

Statements first

Make statements the starting point, with transactions, fees, payments and other activity connected to the relevant statement.

02

Clear status

Make each statement’s status clear, so customers understand its activity and whether they need to take action.

03

Review to payment

Connect the journey from reviewing a balance to choosing a payment method and making a payment.

04

Fees in context

Make fees and rebates easier to understand as part of the overall financial picture.

The tradeoff throughout was simplicity versus completeness: keep the overview calm, without hiding the detail finance teams rely on.

04 · My role

As project lead, I brought together product, commercial and technology partners. I helped shape the experience across statements, transactions and payments, including the structure of the experience and how customers move between reviewing account activity and taking action.

05 · Results

20%
fewer service calls

Fewer customers needed to call to understand a bill or make a payment.

  • A connected statement journey, from balance to transactions to payment
  • Clearer status on every statement, so customers know when to act
  • Fees and rebates explained in context
  • Continued investment as an ongoing, cross-functional program
