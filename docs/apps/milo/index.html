The problem

Most calendars are built for heavy scheduling. Daily coordination for couples, families and small groups needs shared visibility, fast edits and calmer context.

The approach

Milo puts shared calendars, trip activities, invitations and expenses into one plan. People can share a single plan without exposing their whole calendar.

The product

How it came together

01

The problem

Everyday plans turn into coordination work when dates, people, trip details and costs live in different places.

02

The design decision

Make the shared plan the center of the experience, with calendars for organizing and invitations for controlling who sees what.

03

The release

The native iOS release added itineraries and sharing permissions. Later releases added expenses, settlement and plan-specific invites.

Builder notes

What I made and learned

  2. 02
    Built the web app and launch preview so people can try the product instead of only reading about it.
  3. 03
