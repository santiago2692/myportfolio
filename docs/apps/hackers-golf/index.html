The problem

Golfers wanted to make casual rounds more engaging without making scoring complex or slowing down play.

The approach

Player setup, game packs, card rules and quick scoring keep the group experience fun and easy to follow.

The product

How it came together

01

Model the rules

Casual golf-game rules became lightweight setup, scoring and leaderboard flows.

02

Design for the cart

Card and cart-mode interactions built for quick use mid-round.

03

Tune the voice

Iterated the product language so it felt playful without slowing the group down.

Builder notes

What I made and learned

  1. 01
    Turned casual golf rules into a product system for setup, scoring, leaderboards and cart-mode moments.
  2. 02
    Balanced game energy against on-course speed, keeping every interaction short enough for a real round.
  3. 03
    Used the launch as the foundation for spin-offs, including Cards of Chaos.

Outcomes

What shipped

  • Interactive flows for party setup, game-pack browsing and card reading
  • A playful product language that still works on the course
  • Launched through Tiger Mind Labs, with continued iteration after release
