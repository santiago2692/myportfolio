01 · Overview

A login experience can look simple on the surface, but changes to identity and authentication affect multiple products, technical systems, customer-facing teams and communications.

I worked on the customer experience around changes to how WEX customers access their accounts. The work covered usernames and email addresses, multi-factor authentication, passkeys, terminology and the expected login journey.

02 · The challenge

Customers need to understand how to access their accounts and what to do when the experience changes. At the same time, the teams supporting that experience need a shared understanding of customer friction, technical issues, communications and support needs.

03 · Key areas of focus

Where the work focused

01

The whole journey

Look at the complete customer journey, including authentication, communications and support.

02

Clearer language

Make the language around username-to-email changes, MFA and passkeys clearer.

03

One shared picture

Align product, technology and customer-facing teams around the experience.

04

Root causes

Understand the source of friction by looking at trends and root causes: adoption questions, communication gaps, technical issues and gaps in visibility.

The result was a clearer end-to-end journey, plain-language guidance for email sign-in, MFA and passkeys, and one shared view of friction across teams.

04 · My role

As project lead, I worked on the customer experience and communications around the login journey, helping connect perspectives across the teams involved in WOL and OWI.

05 · Results

  • One shared picture of login friction across product, technology and support
  • Clearer language for email sign-in, MFA and passkeys
  • A smoother path through the transition for customers
  • Communications and support aligned to the same journey
