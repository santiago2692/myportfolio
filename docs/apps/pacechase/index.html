PaceChase

A multisport pacing toolkit for running, cycling, swimming, rowing, triathlon and HYROX that turns pace and splits into a finish-time plan.

The problem

Athletes needed a simpler way to calculate pace, forecast finishes and plan splits, without spreadsheet friction.

The approach

PaceChase brings discipline selection, visual pace calculations and multisport predictions into one focused toolkit for planning an effort before race day.

The product

How it came together

01

Translate the math

Pace, distance and finish-time math became simple, athlete-facing calculators.

02

One language, many sports

Modes for running, swimming, triathlon, HYROX and splits share one calculation language.

03

Design for race day

Fast input and readable output, built around confidence: what pace means, where it gets you, and how to adjust.

Builder notes

What I made and learned

  1. 01
    Turned pace math into quick, readable flows for athletes who need answers before and during training.
  2. 02
    Kept different sports under one calculation language instead of fragmenting the experience.
  3. 03
    Focused the launch story on confidence: what pace means and how to adjust.

Outcomes

What shipped

  • A clear flow for finding pace and predicting finish times
  • Race-specific modes for running, swimming, triathlon, HYROX and splits
  • Launched publicly and refined from real athlete use
