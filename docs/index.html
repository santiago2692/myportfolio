How I lead

Fewer parts, clearer decisions, teams that own the outcome.

Invest where traffic and pain overlap

I tie design investment to service volume, retention and revenue, so the team works on what moves the business, not what’s loudest.

Make it real early

Prototypes, not decks, settle roadmap debates. A working flow in front of executives and customers ends arguments faster than any slide.

Grow leaders, not dependencies

I build product and design leaders, create operating models, and help multidisciplinary teams move with ownership and real decision speed.
