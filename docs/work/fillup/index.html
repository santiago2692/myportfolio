01 · Overview

Drivers and managers have different needs, but both depend on timely access to the right information and tools. FillUp brings multiple personas into one app, giving each person an experience relevant to their role.

For drivers, the focus is on supporting them on the road. For managers, it’s on providing access to actions from their phone so they can keep their business moving.

02 · The challenge

A single mobile app needs to work for people with different responsibilities and contexts. A driver on the road may have different priorities from a manager checking in or taking action away from a desk. The challenge is to make one app useful to both audiences while helping each person quickly find what matters to them.
