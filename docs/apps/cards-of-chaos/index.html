The problem

Casual rounds can feel the same after a few holes, and most scoring apps are too serious to create playful group moments.

The approach

Cards of Chaos turns the round into a light card game: draw between holes, reveal a twist and add just enough unpredictability without slowing play.

The product

How it came together

Builder notes

What I made and learned

  1. 01
    Built the product around one fast loop: start a round, pick a deck, draw a twist, keep the group moving.
  2. 02
    Designed the mobile screens, deck economy, card reveal, live rounds, App Store story and product page.
  3. 03
    Pulled the game out of the Hackers Golf universe into a simpler standalone loop.

Outcomes

What shipped

  • Released as a standalone iOS game in the Hackers Golf family
  • Made setup, deck selection and card reveals one mobile flow
  • Added live-round views to keep the group connected
